How HEVI trades

  • 1R is the planned loss at the stop: 10% of equity, never more than 15%. Results are shown in R so they compare across account sizes.
  • A trade needs a reward of at least 3R after fees, slippage and funding, with the target at a real 4H level.
  • Stop-loss and take-profit sit on the exchange from the moment the order fills. Isolated margin, one position at a time.
  • Entries Monday to Thursday (UTC), 48-hour maximum hold, flat by Friday 20:00 UTC.
  • Losing streaks trigger breakers: done for the day after a stop, half risk after two, a 48-hour pause after three.
  • HEVI paper-trades on live prices until its record shows an edge (10+ trades, +0.10R per trade, profit factor 1.2+), then trades real money.
Paper trades are simulated fills on live Hyperliquid prices; live trades are real. Past results don't predict future ones. Not financial advice.
