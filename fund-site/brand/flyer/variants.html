Season one closes at +501%
Season two is already live, and up 144% in fifteen days
In three and a half days at the end of October, a small pool of four members watched each share go from one dollar to six. Kiyoshi, the trading program at the fund's heart, made 471 trades and won 80.9% of them. Every member was paid out: $194.59 in all.
The method is plain. The same stablecoin trades at slightly different prices on Uniswap and on Textile. Every few seconds Kiyoshi compares the two, and when the gap is big enough to pay for itself, it buys on one and sells on the other. The difference goes to the pool.
Profit settles into the share price once a day. Members deposit USDT on Celo from their own wallets, and redeem whenever they like, back to the same wallet. The manager takes 10% of profit above what a member put in, and nothing on deposits.
