How it works
01
Deposit USDC
From your own wallet, on Base. You receive shares at today's price. Your money never leaves the vault contract.
02
Kiyoshi trades the naira
Every few seconds it compares the cNGN price on Uniswap and on Textile. A gap big enough to pay for itself becomes a trade.
03
Profit lands in your shares
Once a day the profit settles into the share price. Every figure is shown in dollars, with its naira value under it at the fund's own rate.
