What happened each week

  • Week 1: a fast start. $114.55 on a small pool. 30 Oct alone made $39.58 from 384 trades. Margin about 4 bps. Share price +50.7%.
  • Week 2: the best week. $216.25. Smart sizing switched on 5 Nov, trades got larger, and 10 Nov (+$83.88, 7.6 bps) and 11 Nov (+$54.07 on $140k, the busiest day) made 38% of the whole season's profit between them. Share price +58.1%.

Reading the weeks

  • Win rate barely moved (76.8% → 76.9% → 74.2% → 69.9% on a tiny sample) while margin per dollar fell to a third and then a tenth. That points at the market, not at Kiyoshi: smaller gaps, or other bots taking them first.
  • Volume fell with it: $492k in week 2, $255k in week 3. Fewer gaps pay for themselves, so fewer trades fire.
  • The gains and the money did not overlap. Almost all of the +149.7% was earned in weeks 1–2, when the pool was $116–$585. Most member money arrived in week 3.

7. What to watch (predictors)

  • Naira volatility and Textile volume: gaps open when the naira moves fast and one venue lags. Today's 1.6% overnight move is the kind of thing to watch for over the next days.
  • Hour and weekday: 13:00–18:00 and 21:00–23:00 Lagos, Tuesday to Friday. Saturday and the early morning are weak.
  • Our size against the Uniswap pool: $400–600 is the sweet spot; over $600 loses margin.
  • Costs against profit: below about $12 a day of profit, the pool pays the gap in running costs.
  • Competition: a falling margin at an unchanged win rate is what other bots look like. We cannot separate it from calm markets without market data.

8. Recommended next steps

  1. Confirm the fee. The vault reads 15%; the flyers say 10%. Either change the flyers to 15% or queue the fee change on chain (it takes effect after 48 hours).
  2. Keep trades at $600 or under. Over $600 wins only 61% at 1.8 bps. If smart sizing can cap there, it protects margin when the pool grows.
  3. Record market data. Save Textile's naira ticker and Uniswap's price every few minutes in the database. In two weeks we can test directly whether volatility and volume predict profit, instead of inferring it.
  4. Decide on the tight first-leg rule. It has been on throughout. The win rate held, so it is not obviously hurting, but its effect cannot be separated from the market's. A clean test costs real money.
  5. Grow member money if you want fee income. Fees come only from members' gains: today that is about $116 of money. In the Quiet case each extra $1,000 of member deposits earns about $5 a month in fees; in the Typical case about $47.
  6. Watch the costs pot. It holds $0.23. At today's profit the 10% carve (about $0.20 a day) does not cover the $1.22 a day of costs, so the pool keeps paying them.

Where these numbers come from

  • Trades: the pool wallet's transactions on Celo, paired into trades (a buy leg and its sale) by the database. Profit is the sale minus the buy, before gas. Days are Lagos days.
  • Share price and fund value: the vault's own settlement history, daily close. The earlier "unit price" in the old off-chain ledger is not used; it does not account for deposits.
  • Members, deposits, redemptions, fees: every Deposited and Redeemed event read from the vault contract, from its first block to today.
  • Markets: Uniswap v3 factory and pool reads on Celo, and Textile's public ticker, at midday UTC on 20 Nov.

What this report cannot tell you

  • Why the margin fell. Calmer markets, other bots, our larger sizes and the tight first-leg rule all fit the data. Only market data over time will separate them.
  • The future. Twenty-three days is a short history, dominated by two spike days. The forecast rows are scenarios, not odds. The Quiet row is my best reading, not a promise.
  • Net returns. Trading profit is before gas. The forecast subtracts running costs and the fee; member returns also depend on when each member joined.
  • The 15% fee on chain. It is read from the vault's current setting. If a change is queued or recent, the earlier redemptions may have used a different rate.
One line to take away. Season 2 earned its +149.7% almost entirely in its first two weeks, and the market has since become much quieter. Kiyoshi is trading as well as before; the plan should assume a quiet market, protect margin by keeping trades at $600 or under, and treat any spike as a bonus.
